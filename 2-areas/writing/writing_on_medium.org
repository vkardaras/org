* How to make money from Medium?

** Medium Partner Program

1. Meet the Medium Partner Program Requirements
2. Apply for the Partner Program
3. Wait for email Confirmation
4. Agree to Terms
5. Set up payouts
6. Link or create Stripe account
7. Complete tax documents
8. Confirm tax information on Stripe
9. Place your existing articles behind Medium's paywall

* How do you get your articles boosted?

** Criteria to boost a story

The curators have stated they're looking for 5 main criteria in a story to qualify it as high quality

- Is the story "constructive"?
- Is the story "original"?
- Does the story author have "relevant experience"?
- Is the story "well-crafted"?
- Is the story "memorable"?

** How to meet the criteria

- Make it useful
- Break new ground
- Give the people what they need
- Draw from your experience
- Craft your content carefully
- Create a lasting impression
